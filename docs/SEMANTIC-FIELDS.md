# Semantic Fields and Hidden Forms

Semantic phrases can now be composed into band, woven, radial, ascending and quartered fields.

A field carries three simultaneous readings:
1. **foreground** — repeated compound phrases;
2. **negative space** — axis, ring, branch or crown-shaped breathing structure;
3. **macro-form** — tree, axis, wing, crown or return-ring trajectory visible at greater viewing distance.

Placements may mirror, rotate, quarter-fold or overlay. These transformations are deterministic and remain tied to the same original phrase IDs and provenance.

This is the first explicit implementation of the Contact/Tesseract principle: one semantic structure can reveal different forms according to scale, spatial organization and transformation.
