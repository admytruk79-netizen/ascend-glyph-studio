# Multiscale Floral Grammar

ASCEND patterns should reveal different information at different viewing distances.

**Micro:** tiny original buds, petals, seeds, leaves, berries, rings, broken rings and geometric rosettes.

**Meso:** those marks organize into vines, floral chains, rosette bands, branch registers, orbital chains and woven fields.

**Macro:** meso structures collectively follow the Tesseract meaning graph, allowing a distant viewer to perceive a larger axis/journey/crown while a close viewer sees intricate botanical detail.

Default reference field: 10 semantic nodes × 24 micro marks = 240 small elements. The generator supports up to 2,560 micro marks per ten-node graph.

This is original procedural geometry. Historical textile/floral research informs compositional intelligence only; museum motifs are not traced.

Design objective: complexity through multitude, not oversized florals. The macro reading must survive while individual micro-elements remain delicate.
