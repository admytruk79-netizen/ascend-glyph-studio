# Krovets composition study and separate field assembly

Rechecked public records kro-537 (KYD255), kro-610 (KYD328) and kro-613 (KYD331), and visually examined their public title previews. All three are catalogued as Poltavshchyna plant-ornament embroidered towels; the latter two explicitly list tree of life and chain stitch. KYD331 also lists Chernihiv oblast, Pryluky district. Administrative oblast and ethnographic region must remain separate. The study manifest records page/preview hashes, attribution, documented technique and visual observations. No subscriber images were accessed.

The previews suggest distinct field organisation: sparse fine branches and differentiated flowers in KYD255, mixed-size radial blossoms in KYD328, and dense curls and round centres in KYD331. These are limited visual observations, not a complete source decomposition or universal regional grammar. Public previews are CC BY-NC-ND; other photos are marked personal/noncommercial. Krovets photographs and traced geometry are not committed or used as source MotifGraphs.

composeReferenceMotifFields reuses layoutReferenceMotifRegion to place multiple independent fields inside one reference envelope. It reserves non-overlapping rectangular fields, preserves local geometry and translated protected spaces, namespaces nodes and relations, retains source provenance, rejects incompatible histories under the same provenance ID, and validates the resulting graph using existing structural/clearance checks. It does not transform canonical glyphs or relax manufacturing gates. Different source minimum gaps are conservatively combined using their maximum.

Three authored study layouts combine separate left/right fields, a main field and a terminal band. Actual geometry comes from the existing permitted Neon plate-37 and partial plate-27 annotations, not the Krovets photographs. Plate-37 side-field presentation is explicitly authored as a 90-degree rotation with its own transformed provenance identity. The open, mixed-scale and denser variants contain 140, 196 and 266 reference elements respectively. Each has zero broken contacts/repeats, protected-space violations, outside-envelope paths and clearance violations under the existing centreline checks. These metrics do not establish realistic thread clearance or machine feasibility.

The gallery is a layout/outline study. It is not a faithful reproduction of the towels, a completed tree-of-life reconstruction, a physical shirt pattern, or a sophisticated finished embroidery design. Continuous border vines, varied new floral forms and detailed interiors remain missing. Krovets object-specific Poltava attribution is not transferred to the Neon geometry. Element counts are not stitch counts.
