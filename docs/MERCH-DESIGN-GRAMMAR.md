# ASCEND Objects — Design Grammar v1

## Principle
The canonical ASCEND glyphs are the vocabulary. Ukrainian regional embroidery traditions provide compositional lessons, not motifs to copy. Kharakternyk material provides a philosophical narrative layer, treated explicitly as folklore/post-folklore rather than literal historical fact.

## Regional grammar translated into ASCEND

### Poltava — restraint / field / breath
Use low-contrast or tonal thread, generous negative space, fine repeated borders, narrow collar/cuff/placket placement. Best fit: Air + Spirit, with Earth as grounding punctuation.

### Hutsul / Kosiv — density / rhythm / mountain energy
Use compact geometric fields, alternating modules, stronger chromatic contrast and stacked border bands. Best fit: Fire + Earth, with Water used as a moving separator.

### Borshchiv / Podillia — weight / protection / threshold
Use dark dense sleeve or cuff zones against a quiet body, broad protective bands, strong terminal marks. Best fit: Earth + Fire. Avoid copying historical motifs; derive all geometry from ASCEND source IDs.

### Bukovyna — richness / material light
Use layered bands, bead/metallic-thread-like accents and controlled surface richness. Best fit: Spirit + Water, with small Fire highlights.

### Polissia — clarity / repetition / continuity
Use spare repeated bands, clear rhythm, limited palette and long uninterrupted sequences. Best fit: Earth + Water.

## Kharakternyk narrative
The Encyclopedia of History of Ukraine describes kharakternyky as a folklore and post-folklore image of Zaporozhian Cossacks endowed in stories with extraordinary or supernatural properties, with collected records appearing from the mid-19th century. We therefore use the tradition as narrative inspiration — discipline, self-command, protection, liminality, courage, relationship to land — without marketing modern inventions as authenticated historical doctrine.

## Product system
1. QUIET LINE — Poltava logic: tonal embroidery, collar/cuff/placket.
2. GUARD LINE — Borshchiv/Podillia logic: dense protective cuff/sleeve bands.
3. RIDGE LINE — Hutsul logic: energetic multi-band geometry.
4. CURRENT LINE — Polissia logic: long rhythmic repeats and flowing separators.
5. THRESHOLD LINE — Bukovyna material logic + Spirit family: premium layered accents.
6. KHARAKTERNYK CAPSULE — disciplined, minimal warrior language: one central canonical mark plus a coded border sequence; never costume.

## Generation rules
- Source geometry is immutable.
- Only verified canonical-digital SVGs may be used in production output.
- Unverified glyphs may appear in internal design exploration only and must be labeled.
- Allowed operations: repeat, translate, rotate, mirror, scale, layer, alternate, space, crop at placement boundaries.
- Forbidden: redraw, smooth, simplify, invent replacement geometry, copy traditional embroidery motifs.
- Every product stores a recipe manifest with source glyph IDs, transforms, family, regional grammar, placement zone, material recipe and version.
