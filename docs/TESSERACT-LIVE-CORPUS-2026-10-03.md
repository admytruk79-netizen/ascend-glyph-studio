## Research corpus started
- Yuliia Nikishenko (2006), *Структура орнаменту в його семантиці*: ornament is modeled as a semantic complex in which configuration and relationships matter; Tesseract therefore rejects one-symbol/one-meaning lookup logic.
- Olexandr Soletskyi (2022), *Emblematic Patterns and Metaphysical Meanings of Hryhorii Skovoroda*: emblematic signification is treated as a useful precedent for pictorial/verbal metalanguage.
- Icon.org.ua Ukrainian Sacred Art research gallery: used as contextual iconographic research organized by schools, subjects, places, and scholarship. Sacred imagery is not automatically eligible for commercial reuse.
