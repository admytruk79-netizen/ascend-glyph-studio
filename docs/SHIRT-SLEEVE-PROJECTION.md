# Shirt Sleeve Projection

Sleeves are first-class Tesseract surfaces.

Zones:
1. **cap** — opening/shoulder field
2. **upper sleeve** — richest narrative/micro-floral field
3. **forearm** — progressive taper and reduced density
4. **cuff** — compressed band/terminal register

Pair modes:
- **mirror** — left/right are bilateral relatives
- **complement** — each sleeve expresses different spatial behavior from the same meaning graph
- **split-macro** — each sleeve carries one half of a larger composition that becomes legible when the arms are positioned together

The intended visual language is multiscale: tiny florals/circles/glyph fragments at close range, meso bands and vines at garment distance, and a larger lineage/ascent structure across the whole sleeve.

Manufacturing dimensions are intentionally not hard-coded here; real pattern-piece geometry and embroidery/print constraints must be supplied downstream before production validation.
