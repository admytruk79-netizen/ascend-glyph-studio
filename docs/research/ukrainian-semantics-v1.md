# Ukrainian material-culture semantics — evidence seed

This corpus is intentionally conservative. It separates **what an object/source actually attests** from historical semantic interpretation and from ASCEND's own design philosophy.

Initial source classes:
- Ivan Honchar Museum catalog records for shirts, pysanky, rushnyky and Kosiv ceramics.
- Encyclopedia of Ukraine reference articles on ornament and embroidery.
- Balushok & Oliynyk (2024), which is particularly important because it cautions that exact original semantics of clothing embroidery are not fully recoverable.

Engine rule: a museum-attested rhombus, star, spiral, bird, pine, oak leaf or cross is evidence that a form occurs in a specific object/region/period. It is **not** by itself evidence for a universal symbolic meaning.

ASCEND abstractions such as Lineage Axis, Journey Band and Dense Carpathian Register are explicitly modern synthesis concepts. They may be informed by documented morphology and composition, but must never be presented as recovered historical doctrine.

Next research passes should add Belarusian embroidery/textiles, Cossack/Kharakternyk material culture with source criticism, Ukrainian woodcarving/metalwork, additional ceramic centers, and region-by-region placement/color/technique evidence.
