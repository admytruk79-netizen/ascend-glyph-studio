# Compound Glyph Language

The engine now treats ornament as language rather than isolated marks.

Hierarchy:
`primitive → original glyph → compound glyph → semantic phrase → band/field → macro-form`

Compound forms currently include flowering, orbital, branching, crown, guardian and flight structures. They are assembled only from the new `asc-new-*` vocabulary and preserve original-synthesis provenance.

A phrase gives compounds semantic intent (root, lineage, path, protection, transformation, flowering, flight, ascent, integration, return) plus cadence. This allows the same meaning to be projected as a sleeve progression, diary border, radial emblem or layered field without copying historical motifs.

The botanical layer therefore becomes grammatical: a tiny flower can be a compound; a chain of compounds can state a phrase; many phrases can resolve into a larger hidden Tesseract form.
