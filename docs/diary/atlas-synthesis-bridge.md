# Atlas → synthesis bridge

The 32 authored atlas fragments remain ASCEND source material.

The redesigned engine does **not** discard them and does **not** treat Earth/Water/Fire/Air/Spirit family labels as the semantic ontology. Family labels remain source-location metadata only.

Diary/Tesseract synthesis may reference 2–5 immutable atlas glyphs, but only after their vectors pass the existing canonical geometry gate. Source paths are never rewritten by synthesis; recipes store source ID/version plus transforms and relationships.

Current repository truth: only `water-02` and `water-03` are marked both `canonical-digital` and `geometry-verified`. Therefore the bridge fails closed when a recipe asks for more than two verified source glyphs. The other atlas fragments remain valuable authored sources but cannot enter production synthesis until their geometry is verified.

This prevents placeholder/generic geometry from silently replacing Oleksandr's authored atlas work while also preventing unverified reconstructions from being presented as canonical.
