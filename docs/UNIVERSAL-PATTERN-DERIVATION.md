# Universal Pattern Language — derivation rule

The language is not Ukrainian, Chinese, Japanese, Indigenous, Islamic, Nordic, or any other historical system. It is newly generated from comparative morphology.

1. Build evidence-backed corpora of traditional pattern systems worldwide.
2. Normalize visual morphology separately from claimed meaning.
3. Discover recurring primitive + relation pairs across multiple independent cultures.
4. Preserve disagreements: a visually similar form may carry different, unknown, or no documented meaning in different traditions.
5. Promote only well-supported cross-cultural structural invariants into the universal construction vocabulary.
6. Assign the new language's semantics explicitly; never pretend the new semantic assignment was the historical meaning of all source traditions.
7. Generate patterns through relational grammar: repetition, alternation, mirroring, rotation, nesting, interlock, branching, borders, centers, interruption, expansion and contraction.
8. Maintain provenance for every derived structural rule.
9. Exclude restricted/sacred material from automatic derivation.
10. Optimize final compositions for textile beauty first: hierarchy, rhythm, density, negative space, multiscale reading and manufacturing constraints.

The goal is a new readable patterned language whose visual DNA comes from structural commonalities observed across world traditions without copying a specific historical artifact.
