# Cultural semantic coverage matrix
The engine must measure gaps rather than assume cultural coverage.

Dimensions:
- culture / region / named tradition
- intended meaning
- object type and garment placement
- technique and material
- evidence quality and independent-source count
- historical vs contemporary/community interpretation
- commercial abstraction permission
- contradiction / ambiguity
- production translation: embroidery, weave, print, leather/tooling
- negative evidence: meanings or motifs that must not be generalized

A culture is **not covered** merely because one motif is present. A culture × meaning cell is production-ready only when it has strong evidence and at least one commercially safe abstractable principle.

Priority meaning matrix: ancestry, protection, longevity, prosperity, healing, courage, transformation, knowledge, journey, return, ascent, freedom, harmony, fertility, hospitality, mourning/remembrance.

Priority coverage expansion: China (regional/tradition-specific), Japan, Korea, India, Central Asia, Caucasus, Middle East, West/East/North/Southern Africa, Indigenous North American traditions only with community-safe evidence, Latin America, Pacific traditions, plus deeper named Ukrainian/Siberian/Western/British corpora.

Never flatten a country into one style. Preserve region/tradition provenance and never infer sacred/restricted meanings from visual resemblance.
